Universal Tally Data Extraction Engine v3.4

Enterprise Accounting & Consolidated Multi-Company Audit Suite
Engineered & Developed By : Ansh Garg
Zero-dependency, high-speed accounting extraction engine for TallyPrime and Tally.ERP 9.

Core Architectural Innovations in v3.4

  • Pure Numeric Signed Net Amount: In the Transaction Register, all "Dr" and "Cr" text strings are eliminated. Every transaction is represented as a pure signed numeric float (Debit as +, Credit as -), enabling instant Excel formulas (=SUM(), =AVERAGE(), Pivot Tables).
  • Active Trial Balance Extract: Replaces flat ledger lists with a comprehensive TB Extract showing the full group hierarchy (e.g. Indirect Expenses > Rent > Office Rent). Signed TB Balance column allows =SUM(...) to compute the exact Trial Balance Total.
  • Strictly 1 Master Excel Workbook: Zero duplicate CSV files. Exactly one multi-tab workbook with Trial Balance Extract, Transaction Register, and Summary.
  • Multi-Company Consolidation: Merge 7 Dua Associates member companies (or all loaded entities) into a single folder and single sheet with Company as Column 1.
  • High-Speed Fast Mode: Targeted XML data projection reduces network payloads by 85%+ for sub-second extracts.
  • Auditor Reconciliation Integrity: Automatically filters out Memorandum, Optional, Cancelled, and Deleted vouchers.
